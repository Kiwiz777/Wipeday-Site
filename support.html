Support

Found a bug, a wrong result, or have an idea? Email US. Screenshots help a lot.

Common questions

A route didn't give the genes it promised in game

Make sure the new plant is in the centre of a large planter and the parents go in about 15 minutes later, around its Sapling stage. Keep other plants of the same type out of range. If it still doesn't match, Facepunch may have changed the rules: please email us the clones and target you used.

Scanning didn't read my genes correctly

Take the picture straight on, with the gene letters sharp and not cut off. You can always fix or add genes by typing them, for example GYGHWX x3.

When is the forced wipe?

Facepunch wipes every server on the first Thursday of each month at 19:00 UK time. The Wipes tab shows it in your own time zone, and you can turn on reminders there.

I'm not getting wipe reminders

Check that notifications for Wipeday are allowed in your phone's settings, and that at least one reminder is turned on in the Wipes tab. Opening the app now and then keeps the next few months scheduled.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Where your local law requires it, the app shows a Privacy & ad choices button in Settings. See the privacy policy for details.

Will my saved plans be lost?

Your data is stored only on your phone, so it stays when you update the app but is removed if you delete the app.
