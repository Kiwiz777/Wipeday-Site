Privacy policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Wipeday is a companion app for the game Rust. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: your game data stays on your phone, and the only third party is Google, which shows the ads that keep the app free.

Data that stays on your phone

Your clones, herds, saved plans and settings are stored only on your device. Wipeday has no accounts and no servers of its own, so this data is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes it.

Camera and photos

Wipeday asks for camera or photo access only when you tap Scan, so it can read plant genes from a picture of your screen. The text is recognized on your device. Pictures are not uploaded and are not kept by the app.

Notifications

If you turn on wipe reminders, they are scheduled on your phone. No server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Advertising

Wipeday shows ads through Google AdMob. Ads start after you've used the app a few times. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • your IP address and approximate location based on it,
  • device information such as model, operating system and language,
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps),
  • your device's advertising identifier, only where you allow it.

On iPhone, the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking in Apple's permission prompt. Wipeday does not currently show that prompt, so ads on iPhone are not personalized with it.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a US state with similar laws, Wipeday asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ad choices in the app.

Google's use of this data is covered by how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools of our own.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or Steam account.

Children

Wipeday is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. Rust itself is rated for mature audiences.

Your rights

Since your game data never leaves your phone, you control it completely. For data Google collects for ads, you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings, and change your ad consent in the app. If you have any questions, email us and we'll help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Bigger changes, such as new features that use a server, will be explained in the app before they take effect.
