Breed better clones. Never miss a wipe.

Wipeday is a companion app for Rust players. It finds the shortest guaranteed route to the plant genes you want, ranks your cows and sheep for breeding, and reminds you before every forced wipe.

Plant gene planner

Add the clones you own and pick a target like 4G 2Y. Wipeday shows each cross as a planter layout, step by step.

Scan your screen

Take a photo or screenshot of your inventory and the genes are read on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.

Animal breeding

Enter your herd's genes and see which pairs give the best odds for the traits you care about.

Wipe countdown

See exactly when the next forced wipe hits in your time zone, and get a reminder a day or an hour before.

Coming soon

Follow the servers you play on and get notified when they wipe or restart.
